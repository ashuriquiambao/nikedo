OUR PROJECT BACKGROUND

About NIKEDO

NIKEDO is our group's Media Technology project, created to explore how a familiar device could be reimagined for the future. This page shares why we chose the laptop, what we hope to achieve, and what we learn through building our prototype.

The Story Behind NIKEDO

For our Media Technology Performance Task, our group chose the laptop as the medium we wanted to innovate. We imagined how laptop design and use might change over the next ten years.

We developed the NIKEDO concept around convenience, creative design, and possible automated functions. We then represented our idea through a DIY physical prototype so others could see and understand the direction of our proposed innovation.

NIKEDO is not just about imagining a future device. It is also about planning a design, working with available materials, and learning how a concept can be represented through a prototype.

OUR CHOICE OF MEDIUM

Why We Chose the Laptop

We selected a familiar piece of technology because it gives us room to explore future improvements while staying connected to everyday life.

📚

Useful in Everyday Life

Laptops are used for schoolwork, communication, research, and many other tasks. This made them a meaningful medium for our project.

🔭

Room for Future Ideas

We wanted to imagine how laptop design and interaction could evolve over the next decade through new concepts and mechanisms.

🛠️

A Prototype We Could Build

A laptop model allowed us to represent our idea physically and show the design through a student-made prototype.

OUR DIRECTION

Mission and Vision

These statements describe the goals and future possibilities that guide our project.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to present a creative laptop innovation concept through a DIY prototype. We aim to explore possible improvements to convenience and design while developing our teamwork, resourcefulness, and problem-solving skills.

🌟 Our Vision

Our vision is to encourage creative thinking about the future of everyday technology. We hope NIKEDO can inspire further ideas and improvements that make technology more convenient and thoughtfully designed.

WHAT WE GAIN

Learning Through Innovation

The process of developing an idea is an important part of our project.

💡

Ideas Into Reality

More Than Just a Laptop Model

Creating NIKEDO gives our group the chance to combine ideas, share responsibilities, and make decisions together. We learn to consider how our design can be represented with the materials and resources available.

We also learn that innovation involves planning, patience, testing, and improvement. A prototype helps us communicate our idea and identify what could be developed further.

These experiences help us appreciate both the creative and practical sides of developing a technology concept.
