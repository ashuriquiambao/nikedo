About NIKEDO

A student innovation project that brings together creativity, technology, and practical thinking to explore a more convenient laptop experience. Discover the idea, purpose, and vision behind our project.

Turning Creative Ideas Into Practical Innovation

NIKEDO is a student-developed DIY laptop innovation that explores how automation and user awareness could improve the way people interact with a laptop.

Our concept focuses on a laptop with an intended automatic opening and closing mechanism, together with a pop-up camera concept and an integrated cooling setup.

More than building a physical model, NIKEDO gives us an opportunity to apply our creativity, learn about technology, and explore how an idea can be developed into a prototype.

OUR DIRECTION

Mission and Vision

These statements describe what we aim to accomplish through the NIKEDO project.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to develop and present a creative laptop prototype that explores automation, convenience, and thoughtful design. Through NIKEDO, we aim to apply our knowledge, work collaboratively, and learn how technology concepts can be developed into practical prototypes.

THE IDEA BEHIND THE PROJECT

How the NIKEDO Concept Works

The project explores a sequence of actions designed to make laptop use more convenient.

01

Side Button

A side button is part of the concept for initiating the laptop's opening mechanism.

02

User Awareness

The concept explores detecting whether a user is present near the laptop.

03

Pop-Up Camera

A camera designed to rise from the laptop is part of the proposed mechanism.

04

Automatic Closing

The intended design explores closing the laptop after a period without detected user presence.

Prototype note: NIKEDO is a DIY prototype and an innovation concept. The automatic actions described above represent the intended design; only features that have been built and successfully demonstrated should be presented as fully operational.
WHY WE BUILT IT

Our Purpose

NIKEDO is about learning through building, testing, and improving an idea.

Innovation Begins With an Idea

We created NIKEDO to explore how a familiar device like a laptop could be reimagined through creative design and automation concepts. By building a DIY model, we can better understand the planning, materials, mechanisms, and technical considerations involved in developing an innovation.

The project also encourages teamwork, resourcefulness, critical thinking, and the confidence to turn an idea into something people can see and understand.
