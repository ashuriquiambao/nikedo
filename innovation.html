Our Innovation

Meet the NIKEDO DIY laptop concept: a design that explores automatic opening and closing, a pop-up camera, and a cooling fan in one project.

About the NIKEDO Laptop

NIKEDO is our DIY laptop innovation. We wanted to explore how a laptop could open and close with less effort while combining useful features in one design. Our prototype includes a laptop body made from simple materials, a side button for the opening mechanism, a pop-up camera concept, and an internal cooling setup.

Purpose of the Innovation

We created NIKEDO to explore a different and more convenient way to use a laptop. The idea is to make opening and closing easier and to bring user detection and cooling into one concept. The prototype helps us present the design and the features we want to develop.

Key Features

Automatic Opening

A button on the side is intended to start the laptop's opening mechanism.

Pop-Up Camera

Before the closing process, the camera mechanism is designed to rise from the back of the laptop.

User Detection

The concept is designed to detect when the user is no longer in front of the laptop.

Automatic Closing

If no user is detected and the waiting time ends, the laptop is intended to close automatically.

Integrated Cooling

The design also includes internal laptop components and a cooling fan in one setup.

Compact Concept

The project explores combining several useful features in one laptop design.

NIKEDO is currently a DIY prototype. Features described as automatic represent the intended design and should only be presented as fully working when demonstrated on the actual model.

How It Is Designed to Work

1. Press the Side Button

The user presses the button on the side to start the opening mechanism.

2. Detect the User

The detection system checks whether someone is still in front of the laptop.

3. Raise the Camera

When no user is detected, the camera at the back is designed to rise before closing begins.

4. Close the Laptop

After the set waiting time, the laptop is intended to close if the user has not returned.

Materials and Tools Used

We used simple materials and tools to build the body and details of our DIY laptop. The list below can be adjusted to match everything used in the actual build.

Materials

  • Cardboard for the laptop body and structure
  • Colored paper for covering and decoration
  • Small sticks for support or structural details
  • Glue sticks for attaching parts
  • Printed keyboard and touchpad designs
  • Printed NIKEDO logo and QR code
  • Motherboard or internal laptop components
  • Cooling fan

Tools

  • Glue gun for joining parts
  • Scissors for cutting paper and materials
  • Cutter for shaping cardboard
  • Ruler for measuring and alignment

Use cutting tools and a hot glue gun carefully, with adult supervision when needed.

Benefits of the Design

The NIKEDO concept explores how automatic opening and closing could make laptop use more convenient. The pop-up camera adds a distinctive feature to the closing sequence, while the cooling fan is included to help manage heat from the internal components. Bringing these ideas together gives us one prototype to demonstrate and improve.

Future Improvements

Our next steps are to improve the opening and closing mechanism, test the user-detection system, and check how the pop-up camera can work safely with the moving parts. We also want to test the cooling fan and internal components, improve the outside finish, and make sure the features work reliably before presenting the concept as a fully functional laptop.
