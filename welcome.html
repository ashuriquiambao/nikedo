Welcome to NIKEDO

Smart Ideas. Creative Design. Better Possibilities.

NIKEDO is a student-developed DIY laptop innovation that explores how creativity and technology can come together to imagine a more convenient computing experience. Our project focuses on an automatic opening and closing concept, a pop-up camera design, and an integrated cooling setup.

Explore Our Innovation → Discover NIKEDO

An Idea Built Around Convenience

NIKEDO reimagines the familiar laptop through a DIY prototype that demonstrates our ideas about automation, user awareness, and practical design.

The concept includes a side button for initiating the opening mechanism, a camera designed to rise from the laptop, and an intended closing sequence when the user is no longer detected.

By developing this project, we explore how an idea can move from planning and material selection to a physical model that can be presented, evaluated, and improved.

THE IDEA BEHIND NIKEDO

More Than a Laptop Concept — A Learning Experience

The main idea behind NIKEDO is to explore how a laptop could respond to user presence and incorporate mechanisms designed for greater convenience. The intended sequence includes opening the laptop, raising the camera, and closing the device after the user has been away for a set period.

Developing the prototype also allows us to explore the materials, design choices, and technical considerations involved in bringing an innovation concept to life.

Development note: NIKEDO is a DIY prototype. Automatic opening, user detection, pop-up camera movement, and automatic closing should be described as working features only if they have been successfully built and demonstrated.
