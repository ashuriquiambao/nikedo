Welcome to NIKEDO

Smart Ideas. Creative Design. Better Possibilities.

NIKEDO is our student-created laptop prototype that explores how familiar technology could be reimagined for the future. Our concept focuses on convenience, creative design, and proposed features for a more responsive laptop experience.

Explore Our Innovation → Discover NIKEDO

Rethinking the Laptop for Tomorrow

We chose the laptop as our medium because it is a familiar technology used for learning, communication, and everyday tasks. We wanted to imagine how its design and features might develop over the next ten years.

NIKEDO turns this idea into a physical DIY prototype, allowing us to present our concept and explore possible improvements to future laptop technology.

OUR FUTURE CONCEPT

Three Ideas Behind NIKEDO

These proposed ideas guide the design of our laptop prototype and represent the direction we want to explore.

⚡

Automatic Movement

A side button and an intended opening mechanism form part of our concept for a laptop that can open with less manual effort.

📷

Pop-Up Camera

We propose a camera that rises from the laptop as part of the opening concept, giving the design a distinctive feature.

🌀

User Awareness and Cooling

The concept also explores user-presence detection for a proposed closing sequence, alongside a cooling fan setup.

OUR PURPOSE

From an Idea to a Physical Prototype

We created NIKEDO to demonstrate how imagination and practical design can be combined in a future technology concept. Our goal is to present a possible laptop innovation, explain the reasons behind our design choices, and show our work through a physical prototype.

This project also gives our group an opportunity to practice teamwork, creative problem-solving, material selection, and prototype development. It allows us to reflect on what can be improved as technology continues to change.

Prototype note: NIKEDO presents proposed future features. Any automatic mechanism or sensor function should be described as operational only if it has actually been built and demonstrated.
